# 📊 E1X Store Sales Performance Analysis

> **Business Question:** Which sales reps, products, and regions are driving revenue — and where are the growth opportunities?

---

## 🗂 Project Overview

Analysed **two years of retail transaction data** (2021–2022) for E1X Store across three regional markets. Built a multi-view analytical framework to identify performance patterns across sales representatives, product categories, and geographic regions — then translated findings into actionable business recommendations.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue Analysed | $19,627 |
| Total Units Sold | 2,121 |
| Orders Processed | 43 |
| Markets Covered | East, Central, West |
| Time Period | Jan 2021 – Dec 2022 |

## 🔍 Analysis Process

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Validated all 43 transaction records for completeness and consistency
- Standardised date formats and regional classifications
- Calculated derived fields: total revenue per order, average order value, monthly trends

### 2. Exploratory Analysis
- Segmented performance by **rep**, **product category**, **region**, and **time period**
- Identified year-over-year revenue growth from 2021 to 2022
- Analysed product-level profitability vs. volume contribution

### 3. Dashboard Development
Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with:
- Revenue trend line (monthly, 2021–2022)
- Regional performance breakdown (map + bar chart)
- Rep performance leaderboard
- Product category contribution (donut + table)

## 📈 Key Findings

| Finding | Insight |
|---|---|
| **Central region dominates** | Central drove **57% of total revenue** despite equal rep allocation |
| **Binders are the top earner** | Binders contributed **~49% of total sales value** despite lower order volume |
| **Revenue grew 12% YoY** | Overall sales improved from 2021 to 2022 |
| **Rep performance gap** | Top rep outperformed bottom rep by over 3x on revenue per order |

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Reallocate rep resources toward Central region** — it consistently outperforms East and West; adding one more rep there would likely yield disproportionate revenue gains
2. **Prioritise Binder inventory and promotions** — high-value, lower-volume product with strong margin contribution; upsell potential is underutilised
3. **Investigate underperforming reps** — the performance gap suggests a coaching or territory issue, not a market problem
4. **Replicate 2022 growth drivers** — identify what changed between 2021 and 2022 and systematise it for 2023 planning
